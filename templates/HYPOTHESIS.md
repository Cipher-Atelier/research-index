# Hypothesis record

Use one record for one bounded claim. Fill the relevant fields; a small hypothesis can live in an issue instead.

- Identifier and short title:
- Author, date, version, and related issue:
- Status: proposed / being tested / supported within stated limits / contradicted / inconclusive / superseded

## Claim and prediction

What do you think is true? What observable result would follow? Describe the scope precisely enough that the test could disagree with you.

## Basis and alternatives

Link the evidence and prior work. Separate observations from interpretation. State assumptions about transcription, segmentation, language, historical context, or model family. List plausible alternatives and the observations that would distinguish them.

For ciphers, identify inherited key values, newly inferred values, uncertain signs, nulls, nomenclature, and editorial repairs separately. Do not present applying a published or surviving key as inventing a new one.

## Test planned before inspecting the result

- Input versions and source locations:
- Method, parameters, and search space:
- Predicted outcome and success measure:
- Counterevidence or failure condition:
- Controls or comparison method:
- Prior exposure to expected text or evaluation material:

If the test was designed after seeing the result, say so. Keep exploratory tests distinct from genuinely unexposed evaluation.

## Outcome and limits

Link the actual outputs, including negative results and alternative parameter choices. Explain what the test supports, contradicts, or leaves undecided. Distinguish mapped positions, internally consistent output, linguistic plausibility, and historical verification. A finite search only speaks to the tested family.

## Review and next step

Who checked which part, using what evidence? State dependencies shared with the original analysis. AI agreement alone is not independent human review. Link corrections, follow-up tests, or the record that supersedes this hypothesis.
