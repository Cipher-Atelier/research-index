# Debosnys: control failure and source comparison, 9 October 2026

The new Cloze3 model failed all six predeclared control thresholds. It was consequently not applied to the real cipher. This is a failed control check of that model, not a negative decipherment of the manuscript, and it supplies no new cipher letters.

A separate comparison of Moore's 1800 and 1869 texts found that the G18/G19 ordering was already present in 1800. G11 differs: Εφιλει in the earlier text against Εφιλε in 1869. The manuscript's M10 agrees with the earlier reading. That agreement does not identify the manuscript's copy-source or prove a decipherment.

The next needed witness is Longworth 1805, volume I, which remains unverified. A Google catalogue result labelled Volume 1 led to a title page marked VOL. II and does not fill that gap. A catalogue label cannot substitute for checking the physical volume and passage.

The supplied locator for the earlier Moore witness is the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/download/odesanacreontra00moorgoog/odesanacreontra00moorgoog.pdf#page=17). The comparison was reported as completed and independently checked; the archive link could not be opened during this publication pass. It is a source locator, not a claim of a fresh visual check here.

This short update reports completed findings supplied for publication. The Moore comparison was also checked against its saved completion record. The original Cloze3 experiment and visual comparison were not rerun for publication; their scripts and full packages are not included. No book, museum image, manuscript crop or private correspondence is reproduced. Codex assisted the summary and checks. No dedicated Debosnys repository has been created.
