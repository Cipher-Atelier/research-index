# Warsaw HCP81: bounded tests and an archival lead, 9 October 2026

The supplied research result concerns the Warsaw telegram dated 26 August 1939, no. 1155, catalogued as [HCP81](https://crypto.hcportal.eu/dashboard/cryptograms/81). It does not establish a plaintext or a new key.

A bounded simple-substitution test used three fresh controls and recovered 450/450 training letters and 300/300 check letters. Eight fixed variants of the real ciphertext produced no coherent reading. Successful control recovery verifies that the tested procedure worked on those controls; it does not identify the real telegram's cipher or exclude every substitution model.

The whole source was subsequently read. The recorded body has 51 five-letter groups, or 255 letters. A separately visible `is` would bring the count to 257 if it belongs to the body; that membership remains qualified. There is no untouched holdout left in the real source. A further test cannot present already inspected material as new blind evidence.

The check of one specific published July indicator table found no matches under the primary reading. The fallback `lsury → 90123 → n=9` is not supported by the independent image reading `laury`. These observations constrain that table and those readings; they do not exclude the whole XQ system. No permutation search or plaintext enumeration was performed.

## Sources and next evidence

Antal, Zajac and Grošek's [HistoCrypt 2020 paper](https://ep.liu.se/ecp/171/004/ecp2020_171_004.pdf), especially the XQ/R description on pp. 22–23, supplies prior scholarship on triple columnar transposition and its indicator procedure. It does not validate the new HCP81 test figures.

Antal, Grošek and Zajac's [Vojenská história article](https://www.vhu.sk/data/att/178/20273.07b25c.pdf), printed p. 97 (PDF page 3), describes the July 1939 XQ distribution including Warsaw. Footnote 10 cites SNA, MZV, box 39, document 5453/1939. This is a lead to the July material and its instructions/annexes, not possession or verification of an applicable Warsaw key.

The [official SNA inventory](https://www.minv.sk/swift_data/source/verejna_sprava/slovensky_narodny_archiv/archivne_pomocky/Ministerstvo_zahranicnych_veci_1939-1945-inventar.pdf) confirms these locations in the MZV 1939–1945 fond:

- Inventory no. 45, box 39, 1939: cipher correspondence, aids and keys (printed p. 24; PDF page 25). Document 5453/1939 is identified by the article, not individually enumerated on this inventory page.
- Inventory no. 428, box 580: Prague material with a cipher-key reference (printed p. 171; PDF page 173). Its applicability to Warsaw is unproved.
- Inventory no. 122, box 208: Warsaw correspondence (printed p. 96; PDF page 97). This is an optional route to seek a parallel text, not an identified plaintext match.

The keys themselves have not been obtained, and no archive request has been sent. The next useful evidence is lawfully accessible, dated key material with documented applicability, or a securely matched parallel text.
